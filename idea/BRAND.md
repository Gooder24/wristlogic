# Brand proposal

> The author picked **Ownbeat** over the recommended Plainbeat on 18 Nov 2026; the other candidates stay as a record.
> Slovak copy stays beside the English because the first users were Slovak.

18 Nov 2026. Nothing here is registered or bought.

## 1. Name

### What I checked, and what I did not

- Web search for apps and brands with the same name (18 Nov 2026).
- Domain registration through the registries' RDAP (`.com` at Verisign, `.app` at Google): "free" means no
  registration record that day. It is not a price or a guarantee.
- **Not checked:** the trademark registers themselves (EUIPO, USPTO, WIPO). Before buying anything, search the
  name in TMview (tmdn.org/tmview) in classes 9, 42 and 44.

### Candidates

| Name | Idea | Clashes found | .com | .app | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Plainbeat** | Your heartbeat, in plain numbers | None found: no app, no brand; not on GitHub, PyPI or npm | free | free | **Recommended** |
| Wristful | A wristful of data you never saw | No product of that name found; many "Wrist…" watch apps | taken | free | Runner-up; leaves out rings and chest straps |
| Rawbeat | The raw signal under the score | None found | taken | free | "Raw" argues against "validated only" |
| Ownbeat | Own what your devices say | "Own Health", a health-data app with a similar name | taken | free | Too close to an existing health app |
| Tepwise | *Tep* is Slovak for pulse | "Tempwise", a smart thermometer brand, one letter away | free | free | Reads as a typo outside Slovakia |
| Beatloom | Signals woven into one | BeatLoom, a multi-device audio sync product using beatloom.com | taken | free | Clash on the same "multi-device" idea |

Dictionary words I tried first and dropped, because both domains are registered: Readout, Spindle, Tare (two
health apps already use it: a weight-cut tracker and a nutrition tracker), Nadir, Latent, Undertone.

### Recommendation: Plainbeat

- It says the promise: plain numbers, plainly sourced, no mystery score.
- It works for a watch, a ring and a strap alike.
- Easy to say in English and in Slovak, no diacritics, one obvious spelling.
- Both domains, the GitHub name and the package names were free on 18 Nov 2026.
- Weak spot: "beat" also suggests music. The tagline and the pixel pulse mark settle it.

## 2. Tagline and positioning

| | EN | SK |
|---|---|---|
| Tagline | Your watch knows more than its app tells you. | Tvoje hodinky vedia viac, než ti ich appka povie. |
| Second line | Every sensor. Every published method. Nothing made up. | Každý senzor. Každá publikovaná metóda. Nič vymyslené. |

**Positioning (EN).** Plainbeat is a local-first health app for people who wear a watch, ring or strap and want
all of what it measures. It reads the device's own data, computes every published metric its sensors can
support, and puts a source and a confidence on each number. Where the data cannot back a number, it shows none.

**Positioning (SK).** Plainbeat je zdravotná appka, ktorá beží u teba, pre ľudí, ktorí nosia hodinky, prsteň alebo
pás a chcú z nich všetko, čo merajú. Číta vlastné dáta zariadenia, vypočíta každú publikovanú metriku, na ktorú
jeho senzory stačia, a ku každému číslu dá zdroj a dôveru. Kde dáta číslo neunesú, neukáže žiadne.

## 3. Tone of voice

Plainbeat talks like a friend who has read the papers: curious, a bit cheeky about unused data, exact about
numbers.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Talk about the data: "Your watch recorded 889 HRV values this month. Its app showed you ten." | Talk about people: no "lazy", "bad developers", "they hide it from you" |
| Say "your watch's app" or name the product as a fact with a source | Call any company bad, or guess its motives |
| Give the number, the method and the year | "AI-powered insights", "optimize", "unlock your potential" |
| Say what is missing and what would fix it | Show a guess to fill a gap |
| Say "above your usual range" | Name an illness, promise detection, give medical advice |
| One joke per screen at most, never about the user's body | Guilt, streaks, confetti |

The playful line is always about the gap between sensor and screen, and it is always true for that user's data.
If we cannot count it in their files, we do not say it.

Slovak: tykanie, short sentences, plain words (appka, hodinky, tep). No English filler where a Slovak word exists.

## 5. Visual direction (text only)

**Idea: the watch's own display, grown to a full screen.** Sports watches show data on a low-resolution,
always-on pixel display. Plainbeat looks like that display, not like a glossy health dashboard. It fits what the
product does (the plain readout).

- **Type.** One bitmap face, Departure Mono, for everything: 11 px body, 22 px titles, 44 px for the one big
  number on a screen. No second font, no weights; size and colour carry the hierarchy.
- **Colour.** One dark palette, nothing else: surface `#000a07`, panels `#0a1512` to
  `#26302c`, text `#f0f0eb`, muted `#b4b4b0`, one accent `#628790`. State colours only for state: `#a1d289`
  within range, `#e2c08d` outside range, `#e5786d` failed import. A light theme uses the same tokens inverted,
   No gradients, no glow, no shadows.
- **Shape.** Rounded panels and buttons; only the pixel art itself is square. 2 px
  borders.
- **Charts.** Drawn on the pixel grid: stepped lines, no smoothing, no area gradients. The personal baseline band
  is a dithered strip behind the line. A second device is a thinner line in the muted colour. Missing data is an
  empty gap, never an interpolated line.
- **Confidence you can see.** Solid fill for A, dithered fill for B, outline only for C, each with its letter
  and one word beside it. Colour is never the only carrier.
- **Locked metrics.** A tile filled with as many pixel cells as there are nights collected: 9 of 14 lit. It is a
  still picture that changes once a day. No progress animation, no shimmer.
- **Device card.** A pixel drawing of the device class (watch, ring, strap) on the 24 px grid, with each sensor
  as a labelled row under it: icon plus one word, never an icon alone. Icons come from pixelarticons; a missing
  one is drawn on the same grid.
- **Mark.** A flat pixel line with one single step up and back down: a heartbeat with nothing added. It works at
  16 px as the favicon and in one colour.
- **Motion.** None that keeps running. A number changes by being replaced.
- **What it must not look like.** Activity rings, glassy cards, a purple or neon gradient, a "score of the day"
  hero with three cards under it, stock photos of runners.
